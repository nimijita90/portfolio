# WAND case study brief

Source: `New Assets Portfolio/WAND.pdf`

Status: content foundation complete. Public visuals require review.

## Core story

WAND was GiG's white-label casino platform. María did not create the original product. She inherited a live system whose design source no longer matched the product and helped evolve it as the platform, client base and business scaled.

The case is not about building a design system from scratch. It is about evolving an incomplete and rigid foundation into infrastructure that helped teams sell, preview, customise, design, implement and launch casino brands more efficiently.

## Role and period

- Period: 2019-2025.
- Role: Product Designer, later taking ownership of WAND's design direction and progressing into Design Leadership.
- Collaboration: Product Design, Graphic Design, Product, Engineering, Sales, clients and stakeholders.

## Initial problems

- Design did not fully represent the live product.
- Pages, flows, states, edge cases, tablet layouts and components were missing or outdated.
- The original model could not support the range of client navigation, theme and casino requirements.
- A standard branded skin could take about 1-2 weeks.
- Client asset handoff was unclear and created unnecessary waiting and rework.
- Design and production implementation were becoming disconnected.

## Strategy

### Complete the product

Make Design a reliable representation of the live product. Complete components, pages, flows, states and realistic scenarios across desktop, mobile and tablet.

### Make it configurable

Replace repeated manual rebuilding with controlled configuration. Use variables for themes, currencies, languages, content, navigation, component choices and casino models.

### Connect the workflow

Extend the system beyond Product Design so Sales could prepare realistic previews, clients could understand customisation and required assets, and Engineering could receive clearer specifications.

## Decisions worth showing

- Completing the source of truth instead of treating the library as the product.
- Supporting top navigation while retaining the existing sidebar model.
- Reworking colour roles so dark experiences could function in both Figma and production.
- Extending WAND from Real Money Casino to Sweepstakes without creating a separate product source.
- Turning repeated skinning work into configuration through Figma Variables.
- Improving asset guidance and client handoff with Graphic Design.
- Protecting shared UX quality when client-specific features were considered for the wider catalogue.
- Recognising when further evolution was no longer the right answer and transitioning toward XSITE.

## Verified impact

- 43 brands designed or pitched.
- 22 casino brands launched.
- 15+ markets reached.
- Standard skin turnaround reduced from about 1-2 weeks to about 3 days. Additional customisation could increase this time.
- Earlier client feedback before expensive implementation.
- More realistic commercial previews.
- Better alignment between Design and Engineering.
- Clearer client onboarding and asset requirements.
- Less repetitive page-by-page work.

Do not invent engagement, conversion or revenue metrics.
