# V9 storyboard

Design read: an editorial product-leadership portfolio for hiring managers and senior product leaders. The visual language is cinematic, dark and image-led, with real product evidence replacing generic case-study cards.

Design dials:

- Design variance: 8.
- Motion intensity: 7.
- Visual density: 3.

Theme: near-black, ivory and photographic charcoal with one controlled accent drawn from the approved portfolio imagery. Sharp editorial surfaces, with rounded geometry reserved for device frames and controls.

## Home

### Hero

A portrait-led first viewport with María's name, role and one concise positioning statement. Award and experience stay secondary. The reveal establishes hierarchy through a controlled portrait aperture and staggered type, with a static reduced-motion state.

### Highlights and operators

Large verified figures sit in an asymmetric field. Operator logos use the page's single horizontal marquee. Mobile changes to a swipeable logo rail and stacked figures.

### Product expertise

A full-viewport typographic sequence cycles through the five expertise areas. Motion communicates the breadth of one connected product discipline, not decoration.

### Career journey

A three-state progression from Graphic Designer to Product Designer to Design Leader. The active state changes through direct controls and scroll entry. Mobile uses a simple vertical sequence with no pinning.

### Selected work

WAND becomes the first real case and receives a full-width editorial feature rather than a card. It shows the thesis, role, period and verified scale, plus one approved product visual. The case remains preview-only until confidentiality is reviewed.

Future cases enter beneath WAND only when their source material is ready. XSITE is the narrative next step.

### International recognition

One strong award photograph anchors a sparse recognition sequence. Awards appear as large date and result pairings, without repeating card containers.

### What people say

Verified recommendations orbit a real portrait composition on desktop. Mobile becomes a readable vertical quote sequence. Quotes stay short and preserve attribution.

### FAQ and videos

Six questions share one active video surface. Selecting a question swaps the video, transcript and poster frame. Keyboard operation, captions and reduced motion are required.

### Beyond design

An irregular photo mosaic introduces personal material without labels over the images. Mobile uses one strong image followed by a compact swipe gallery.

### Contact

A monumental email action closes the page. The same contact wording is used everywhere to avoid duplicate intent.

## WAND case

The source material is distilled into seven web acts so the case remains scannable.

### Cover

WAND, the thesis, period, role and verified scale. One approved multi-device product visual.

### Context and ownership

Explain the existing white-label platform and María's progression from hands-on product design into ownership of design direction. Use a compact 2019-2025 timeline.

### Scale exposed the cracks

Group the initial problems into three ideas: incomplete source of truth, rigid foundations and manual delivery. Use real evidence only.

### Complete, configure, connect

The strategic core. Three visually distinct but connected moments show the source of truth, the configuration model and the cross-functional workflow.

### Decisions in the product

Use a small number of deep examples rather than a gallery:

- Navigation models.
- Dark-mode colour architecture.
- Figma Variables and configuration.
- Sweepstakes support.

The dark-mode example is the primary cross-functional leadership story.

### Workflow and design judgement

Show how earlier previews, clearer client handoff and UX quality control connected Design with Sales, Graphic Design, Product and Engineering.

### Impact and transition

Lead with the verified turnaround change and support it with brand, launch and market figures. Close honestly: WAND reached its architectural limits, and XSITE became the next chapter.

## Visibility rules

- The home summary can use approved text and verified figures.
- Product screenshots stay private until each asset is classified.
- Redacted material must be designed as redacted, not blurred at the last moment.
- Protected content needs an explicit access decision before a detailed route is published.
- No fabricated historic states, before-and-after screens or product outcomes.

## Motion rules

- Hero motion communicates arrival and hierarchy.
- Expertise motion communicates breadth.
- Career motion communicates progression.
- WAND configuration motion communicates state change.
- All other sections use short entry transitions only.
- Every motion sequence has a static reduced-motion equivalent.
